# Corporate Information

## Overview

Corporate Information includes information used to establish the legitimacy, ownership, management, and operational structure of an organization.

It forms the foundation of most due diligence investigations.

---

## Common Examples

- Corporate Registration Records
- Company History
- Leadership Information
- Shareholder Information
- Beneficial Ownership Records
- Organizational Structure
- Capital Asset Documentation
- Business Continuity Plans

---

## Sensitivity Classification

**Sensitivity Level:** Medium

Although less sensitive than personal information, corporate information may contain confidential business information and ownership details that require protection.

---

## Why It Matters

Corporate information is used to determine:

- Whether the entity legally exists
- Who owns and controls the organization
- Whether ownership structures present risk
- Whether the organization can meet contractual obligations

---

## Recommended Governance Approach

Organizations should:

- Verify information using authoritative sources
- Maintain documentation supporting verification decisions
- Periodically review ownership and leadership information
- Retain records according to established schedules

---

## Recommended Security Controls

### Data Protection

- Encryption

### Governance

- Vendor Risk Assessment

### Monitoring

- Audit Logging
- Security Monitoring
