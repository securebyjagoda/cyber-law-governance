# Identity & Personal Information

## Overview

Identity & Personal Information refers to information that can identify, relate to, describe, or reasonably be associated with an individual.

This category often contains the most heavily regulated information collected during due diligence and background check processes due to its potential impact on privacy, identity theft, and fraud.

---

## Common Examples

- Full Name
- Date of Birth
- Residential Address
- Email Address
- Phone Number
- Social Security Number (SSN)
- Driver's License Number
- Passport Information
- State-Issued Identification
- Beneficial Ownership Information

---

## Sensitivity Classification

**Sensitivity Level:** High

Identity information is frequently targeted by attackers because it can be used for:

- Identity Theft
- Account Takeover
- Social Engineering
- Fraudulent Transactions
- Unauthorized Access

---

## Key Regulatory Considerations

Identity information may qualify as:

- Personal Information under 201 CMR 17.00
- Personal Data under GDPR
- Consumer Information under various state and federal regulations

---

## Recommended Governance Approach

Organizations should:

- Collect only what is necessary
- Document a lawful purpose
- Establish retention limits
- Restrict access to authorized personnel
- Include identity information within the WISP
