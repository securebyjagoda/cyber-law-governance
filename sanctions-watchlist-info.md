# Sanctions & Watchlist Information

## Overview

Sanctions & Watchlist Information is used to identify restricted, prohibited, or high-risk individuals and organizations.

Organizations use screening processes to comply with sanctions, anti-money laundering, and financial crime obligations.

---

## Common Examples

- OFAC Screening Results
- EU Sanctions Results
- UN Sanctions Records
- Politically Exposed Persons (PEP)
- Adverse Media Information

---

## Sensitivity Classification

**Sensitivity Level:** High

Although largely derived from public sources, sanctions information may significantly impact eligibility decisions and therefore requires careful handling.

---

## Why It Matters

Screening helps organizations:

- Comply with sanctions laws
- Reduce financial crime risk
- Identify high-risk associations
- Support risk-based decision-making

---

## Recommended Governance Approach

Organizations should:

- Establish escalation procedures
- Document screening decisions
- Maintain continuous monitoring programs
- Periodically re-screen parties

---

## Recommended Security Controls

### Data Protection

- Encryption
- Access Control

### Monitoring

- Security Monitoring
- Audit Logging

### Governance

- Continuous Monitoring Program
- Vendor Risk Assessment
