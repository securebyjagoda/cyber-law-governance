# Criminal & Background Information

## Overview

Criminal & Background Information consists of information used to assess legal exposure, past misconduct, litigation, and criminal history associated with a third party.

This is often one of the most sensitive categories processed during background checks.

---

## Common Examples

- Criminal Records
- Court Filings
- Litigation History
- Complaints and Claims
- Government Investigations
- Regulatory Enforcement Actions

---

## Sensitivity Classification

**Sensitivity Level:** Very High

Improper processing of criminal and legal information can create significant privacy, regulatory, and reputational risks.

---

## Why It Matters

Organizations use this information to:

- Identify misconduct risks
- Evaluate legal exposure
- Assess reputational concerns
- Make risk-based decisions

---

## Special Considerations

Criminal records are subject to heightened protections in many jurisdictions.

Organizations should ensure:

- The information is necessary
- The information is accurate
- Decisions involving criminal records include human review
- Retention periods are documented

---

## Recommended Governance Approach

- Conduct a DPIA when appropriate
- Document lawful basis for processing
- Maintain human review procedures
- Establish retention requirements

---

## Recommended Security Controls

### Data Protection

- Encryption
- Access Control

### Governance

- Data Retention Schedule
- Human Review Procedure

### Monitoring

- Audit Logging
- Security Monitoring
- Data Loss Prevention (DLP)
