# Legal & Regulatory Information

## Overview

Legal & Regulatory Information consists of records used to evaluate compliance, legal obligations, contractual relationships, and regulatory history.

---

## Common Examples

- Contracts
- Partnership Agreements
- License Agreements
- Regulatory Filings
- Government Audits
- Tax Filings
- Compliance Documentation

---

## Sensitivity Classification

**Sensitivity Level:** High

These records frequently contain confidential business information, contractual obligations, and legal strategies.

---

## Why It Matters

This information helps determine:

- Legal compliance
- Existing liabilities
- Regulatory exposure
- Contractual obligations

---

## Recommended Governance Approach

Organizations should:

- Maintain appropriate retention schedules
- Restrict access based on business need
- Document legal review procedures
- Preserve audit trails

---

## Recommended Security Controls

### Data Protection

- Encryption
- Access Control

### Monitoring

- Audit Logging
- Security Monitoring

### Governance

- Records Management Program
- Vendor Risk Assessment

---

## Related Workflow Phases

- Verification
- Criminal & Legal Checks
