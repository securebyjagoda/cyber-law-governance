Massachusetts Laws

  • 201 CMR 17.00: WISP and security safeguards

Key Finding

GDPR governs both the collection and protection of personal information throughout the transaction lifecycle, while Massachusetts laws focus primarily on security controls protecting information after collection.

GDPR

  • Article 33: Breach notification

Massachusetts Laws

  • 201 CMR 17.00: WISP, vendor oversight, and security safeguards
  • M.G.L. c.93H: Breach notification obligations
Key Finding

Both GDPR and Massachusetts laws emphasize cybersecurity controls and risk management; however, GDPR Article 28 imposes significantly more detailed contractual oversight and accountability requirements for third-party providers.
